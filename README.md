# Google Advanced Data Analytics Capstone

<div align='center', style="font-size: 48px"> <strong>Predictive Workforce Analytics: An XGBoost Model for Employee Attrition Identification<strong> </div>

## Overview
### The Company: Salifort Motors
    Salifort Motors is a global-workforce of over 100,000 employees research, design, construct, validate, and distribute electric, solar, algae, and hydrogen-based vehicles. Salifort’s end-to-end vertical integration model has made it a global leader at the intersection of alternative energy and automobiles.

### Business Problem:
    A high employee turnover concerns the Leadership Team of Salifort Motors and wants to identify factors that influence employee churn and asks for data-driven suggestions.

## Deliverables
    1. Project Proposal
    2. PACE Strategy Documentation
    3. Exploratory Data Analysis
    4. An XGBoost model that forecasts employee attrition based on employee metrics and supplemental information
    5. Model evaluation and results
    6. Executive Summary Document

## Dataset
### [Hr Analytics Job Prediction](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mfaisalqureshi/hr-analytics-and-job-prediction?select=HR_comma_sep.csv)

**_Publisher:_** [Faisal Qureshi](https://www.kaggle.com/mfaisalqureshi)

**_Data Dictionary_**

Variable|Description
---|---
satisfaction_level|Employee-reported job satisfaction level [0&ndash;1]|
last_evaluation|Score of employee's last performance review [0&ndash;1]|
number_project|Number of projects employee contributes to|
average_monthly_hours|Average number of hours employee worked per month|
time_spend_company|How long the employee has been with the company (years)
Work_accident|Whether or not the employee experienced an accident while at work
left|Whether or not the employee left the company
promotion_last_5years|Whether or not the employee was promoted in the last 5 years
Department|The employee's department
salary|The employee's salary (U.S. dollars)
